This is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the Instagram account @{{IG_USERNAME}}. It sends a direct message to people who comment a specific keyword on that account's posts, so they receive the link or resource they asked for.

Information the tool processes

  • Comments posted on the account's own posts: comment text, comment ID, and the commenter's Instagram username and ID.
  • The ID of the post the comment was left on.

It does not collect passwords, payment information, or any data from other parts of a user's Instagram profile.

How the information is used

Only to (1) detect whether a comment contains a configured keyword, (2) send one automated reply message to that commenter, and (3) reply publicly to the comment. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sold or shared with third parties.

Storage and retention

Only comment IDs are stored (to avoid sending duplicate messages) and are automatically deleted after 8 days. Message content is not stored.

Third parties

The tool communicates only with Meta's Instagram API and runs on Cloudflare Workers. Data is subject to their respective policies.

Your choices and data deletion
